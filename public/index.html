      About RailTrack

      Real-time Indian Railways tracker architecture and integrity.

      Dual-Engine Transit Architecture

      Powered by RailEngine & RailCore APIs with zero data fabrication.

      Zero Data Fabrication

      RailTrack shows genuine data from verified railway developer platforms. Missing fields are hidden or marked unavailable. Train positions between station updates are smooth interpolated estimates based on official timetables and ETA, not raw GPS fixes.

      Overhead 25kV Electric Traction

      Visualizations simulate high-speed electric locomotive traction with overhead catenary lines, pantograph contact shoes, dual steel rails, concrete sleepers, and dynamic headlight illumination.

      Dual-Engine Reliability & Caching

      API keys are securely held server-side. Redundant failover between RailEngine and RailCore ensures route timetables, live station boards, and real-time telemetry always load cleanly without rate limits.
